  Registered: each s0 run's submitted Sharpe is placed within its own replay
  null; the position is the certifying null's p-value for that run. If the
  gate reproduces what the agent does on noise, those positions are uniform.
  Rule (validity, one-sided): FAILS HIGH iff the LOWER end of the Wilson 95%
  interval for the type-I rate exceeds nominal, at alpha = 0.05 and 0.01.
    Holds: no gross leakage, and the per-kind fractions are reported flat.
    Fails high: the per-kind fractions name the suspect kind, and NO
      behavioural readout from the cell is reported as a finding until it is
      understood.

      departure with the ECDF, not as a size failure.

  RANDOM.

  20 of 80 runs carry no certifying-null p-value, so
  they have no position to place. `quixote/certify.py` does not price the
  certifying null on a run with a logged trigger change: the pre-change
  rule is what replays, so the search after the change is a decision the
  null cannot price, and the verdict is DEPENDS_ON_JUDGMENT with the
  fixed-sequence null reported as the bracket's liberal end instead.
    20 run(s): status DEPENDS_ON_JUDGMENT, triggers_changed=True
    indices: [1, 6, 8, 12, 17, 18, 20, 23, 27, 36, 39, 48, 55, 61, 64, 65, 66, 74, 77, 79]

  WHY THIS IS NOT A MATTER OF READING AT A SMALLER n. The missing runs are
  exactly the runs in which the AGENT CHANGED A RULE. Reading the
  remaining ones conditions the uniformity check on agent behaviour — on
  'runs that did not change a trigger' — which is not the population
  check 1 registered, and the direction of any resulting bias is not
  known. The detectability statement registered with the check ('at
  n = 80 this detects a true rate near 13% or worse') does not
  hold at a smaller n either.

  NOTHING IS READ. Check 1 remains unread, and a decision is needed before
  it can be: read at the reduced n with the exclusion recorded as a
  deviation and the conditioning stated; or register how a bracketed run's
  position is to be defined; or record check 1 as unreadable on this arm.
