# E17: is the naive bootstrap's distortion graded in how strongly the anchor tracks performance?

Committed before running. Code: `experiments/e17_graded_coupling.py`.

## Question

e15 showed that anchoring round 2 on the round-1 winner inflates naive type-I
while random and loser anchors do not, and E16 showed the mechanism is the
order-statistic dominance behind the note's P4. Is the distortion specific to
the exact winner, or does it grow smoothly with how strongly the anchor
tracks performance?

## Design

- Searcher: `GumbelAnchored(tau)` (`searchers/dose_response.py`),
  `max_features=2`. The round-2 anchor is drawn with probability proportional
  to exp(tau · z_k), where z_k is feature k's single-feature Sharpe
  standardized across features; the Gumbel noise comes from the searcher's
  seed, so replays reuse it.
- tau ∈ {−∞, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 4, +∞}. −∞ is the loser anchor, 0 is uniform
  random (a data-oblivious menu), +∞ is the winner anchor.
- Configuration (e15's): K=20, M=50, T=500, ρ=0.3 equicorrelated, s=0,
  sigma=1, B=1500.
- n=500 draws, seeds 30000–30499, paired across all tau levels: every level
  sees the same data and the same resampled time indices at each seed.
- `sr_sel`: the searcher's own `replay` value on the real data.
- Three nulls per draw, all with one block length (`select_block_length` on
  the demeaned base columns) and bootstrap seed = the draw's seed:
  - **naive:** `estimator.bootstrap.null_max_bootstrap` on the logged transcript;
  - **recursive:** `estimator.recursive_bootstrap.recursive_null_max_bootstrap`;
  - **full-class:** `null_max_bootstrap` on `estimator.full_class.full_class_matrix(base, 2)`,
    all 210 feature subsets of size at most 2.
- Coupling κ per draw: `normalized_rank(base, anchor)` on the real data (1 for
  the best single-feature Sharpe, 0 for the worst). This is the same κ the
  planned winner-chasing audit measures from a transcript, so E17 is also that
  audit's validation.
- Recorded per draw: the three p-values, κ, anchor, winner, `sr_sel`, block
  length. Git commit and dirty state are captured at launch.

## Predictions

- κ rises monotonically with tau (by construction).
- Naive type-I rises monotonically with tau; at or below 5% for tau ≤ 0;
  tau = +∞ reproduces e15/E16 (roughly 8–10%).
- Recursive and full-class type-I are at or below about 5% at every tau.

## Decision rule

Primary: a one-sided trend test, across tau levels, of the naive rejection
indicators (a draw rejects when its naive p-value is below α = 0.05). Statistic: the sum over draws and levels of the level's
rank (1 for −∞ through 8 for +∞) times the rejection indicator. Null
distribution: permute the eight indicators within each draw independently,
10,000 permutations, which respects the pairing. p = (1 + #{permuted ≥
observed}) / 10,001.

- **p < 0.01:** the note claims graded coupling.
- **p ≥ 0.01:** the note claims winner-anchoring only, with worst-anchoring as
  its mirror.

Secondary, reported but not deciding the claim: per-tau type-I with Wilson
95% intervals and KS tests for all three nulls; the same trend test on the
recursive and full-class indicators; mean κ per tau.
