Privacy notice · draft for review

Privacy

Everything is computed in your browser. The gate runs in a Web Worker on your device. The files it audits are the bundled examples or the files you load, read in your browser; nothing you do here is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no third-party trackers, no advertising.
  • No storage of your data: this site writes nothing to your browser's storage and keeps nothing on a server.
  • No third-party requests: every script, font, WebAssembly file and Python package is served from this site. The one exception is the model calls you start on the run page, which go from your browser to the provider you chose.

What the host sees

The site is static and served by a hosting provider (Vercel), which, like any web server, processes your IP address and request details to deliver the pages and may keep short-lived access logs under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or use these logs to identify anyone.

Running your own agent

When you run your own agent, your API key is sent from your browser directly to the model provider you choose (Anthropic), held in the page's memory for that run only, and never sent to us, stored or logged. The panel and the class you load are read in your browser and not uploaded. What the model sees is what the harness shows it: the moves, their results and the feature names, sent to the provider under its own terms. A share link carries the verdict and the hashes in the part of the address after #, which browsers do not send to any server.
