Privacy notice · draft for review

Privacy

Everything is computed in your browser. The gate runs in a Web Worker on your device. The files it audits on this page are the bundled examples; nothing you do here is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no third-party trackers, no advertising.
  • No storage of your data: this site writes nothing to your browser's storage and keeps nothing on a server.
  • No third-party requests: every script, font, WebAssembly file and Python package is served from this site.

What the host sees

The site is static and served by a hosting provider (Vercel), which, like any web server, processes your IP address and request details to deliver the pages and may keep short-lived access logs under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or use these logs to identify anyone.

Later phases

If a later version lets you run your own agent, your API key will be sent from your browser directly to the model provider you choose, held in memory for that run only, and never sent to us, stored or logged. Uploaded files will be read in your browser and not uploaded. This notice will be updated before any such feature is offered.

Contact
