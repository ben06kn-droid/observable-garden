The Observable Garden: fixed and adaptive candidate sets under the DSR

Knaster (2026). On SSRN: papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7616559.

A backtest is usually the best of many attempts, so its Sharpe ratio has to be judged against what the best of many attempts would find on pure noise. When the search is adaptive, testing against the list of strategies it happened to try gives the wrong error rate. A class of strategies declared before the search, or a log of every decision that can be re-run on noise, restores a valid test. Quixote is the gate built on that answer.

Where it stands

The gate keeps its error rate in every registered test; its limit is power. On real data it refused nearly everything, including 240 LLM agent sessions on liquid ETFs, and issued one certificate, on a long history of industry portfolios, whose lower bound held on the six years that followed. It is evidence that the gate works, not a source of trading signals.
