A backtest is the best of many attempts.

Quixote is an overfitting gate for agent-found strategies. It logs the search, replays it on edgeless resamples, and passes a rule only if it clears that null. It runs in your browser.

Try it on three bundled searches in the demo, or bring your own on Run. Details in the paper.
